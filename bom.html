ASHA Sahayak — offline Hindi voice assistant, prototype unit

Build of record · 20 August 2026
Quantities are per one assembled unit
Costs in ₹ INR, inclusive of 18% GST

The prototype contains no custom hardware. Every part is an off-the-shelf module bought from a distributor and joined with jumper leads — a deliberate choice, so the engineering effort went into the software stack rather than into a board spin we would have had to redo once the software settled. The custom items are listed separately as the production step.

Why nothing here is custom yet

A custom carrier board designed before the software was finished would have been wrong twice: the voice build does not need the camera for capture, and the trigger pin turned out to need input conditioning the reference carrier does not provide — it cannot hold a sustained press, which is why recording is a toggle rather than hold-to-talk. Both were found by running the real workload on off-the-shelf parts. The production board below is specified because those answers are now known — it fixes the pull-down in the pinmux, integrates an audio path that is currently three separate USB devices, and keeps a camera interface so the screening SKU is a populate option rather than a second board.

03Notes

  • No radio is used. The developer kit's M.2 Wi-Fi/Bluetooth card is present and enumerates, but the application never opens a socket and the device is airplane-mode capable by construction. The production carrier omits the M.2 slot entirely.
  • Vision SKU. The production carrier retains a camera interface. Populating it with an 8 MP autofocus module adds roughly ₹900 at volume. It is deliberately not in the ₹12,000 base: no camera ships until a screening aid has cleared Ministry of Health and ICMR validation, and the device presents any such result as a reason to refer, never as a diagnosis.
  • Prices are single-unit Indian distributor prices as of August 2026, inclusive of 18% GST; the Jetson kit is listed at ₹31,959 ex-GST. Production figures are targets based on component pricing at 10,000 units, not quotations.
  • Nothing in the software is tied to this hardware. Speech, translation and synthesis run through ONNX Runtime and CTranslate2, and the language model through llama.cpp — all with working CPU paths. That is what makes the SoM line above substitutable, and it is the single largest lever on unit cost.
