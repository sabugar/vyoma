Every pregnant woman and every newborn in India is in scope

This does not serve all 147 crore Indians, and we do not claim it does. Today's scope is maternal and newborn care, so the people it reaches are mothers and their children — about 2.3 crore births a year, and the women carrying them. An ASHA is the first and often the only health worker present at the home for most of those births. Maternal mortality is 88 per lakh live births (SRS 2021–23) and neonatal mortality 19 per 1,000 (SRS 2023): roughly 20,000 mothers and 4.4 lakh newborns a year. Most newborn deaths fall in the first 48 hours — exactly when an ASHA is in the home, the decision is hers, and her only reference is a book she cannot search. That is the scope today, and it is also the entry point: the same device at the same ₹0 per question is how basic medical knowledge eventually reaches every Indian in their own language.

02Target market

Three parties, deliberately distinct: NHM or the district health society buys, the ASHA uses, the mother and newborn benefit. About 9 in 10 of the 10.2 lakh ASHAs work rural blocks, where a cloud assistant is not an option at all.

It assists the ASHA; it replaces neither her nor what is already deployed. Frontline apps such as Gujarat's TeCHO+ schedule the visit and capture the data — they tell her who to see. This answers the clinical question at the doorstep: what to do once she is there.

One device covers all of India. Each unit carries one language pack, and BHASHINI already publishes ASR, translation and speech for every scheduled language. A new state is a model swap on the same hardware — not a new build.

03Revenue model

Government procurement through NHM. ASHAs are an existing cadre with a budget line for kits and training; the device is sold into that line, in two parts:

  • Device sale — one-time, per unit, to the state or district health society.
  • Annual content & support — revisions, new protocols, replacement. Recurring, and what keeps answers correct as guidelines change.

NGO and CSR-funded deployments are the same product on a different purchase order — how the first pilots get paid for.

05Path to scale

Three things scale independently on the same device — what it knows, what it can sense, what language it speaks. None needs new hardware: the production carrier already carries a camera interface (populating it is a ₹900 option) and the language packs already exist at BHASHINI. The camera path is gated on approval, not on our own confidence — we do not intend to choose the conditions ourselves, and nothing visual ships as a health claim until it clears the Ministry of Health and ICMR route. It will always present a reason to refer, never a result.
