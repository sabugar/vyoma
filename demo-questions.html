These ten were not chosen for how well they read. They were chosen by running twenty-six candidate questions through the device and keeping only those whose answers are correct and traceable to a page of the ASHA manual. Sixteen were discarded — among them a question about bleeding after delivery, which the device answered from the contraception page because Module 7 does not cover postpartum haemorrhage at all. 01Baby does not breathe at birth

बच्चा पैदा होने के बाद रो नहीं रहा है और साँस भी नहीं ले रहा तो क्या करें

Why it mattersBirth asphyxia is the single commonest cause of newborn death in the first minutes of life. The window to act is about a minute, and the ASHA is usually the only person present.

What the device says

यदि बच्चा रो नहीं रहा है और जन्म के बाद सांस भी नहीं ले रहा है, तो तुरंत एक बलगम निकालने वाले से गले को चूसें। यदि बच्चा अभी भी सांस नहीं लेता है, तो श्लेष्मा निकालने वाले से नाक को चूसो। यदि बच्चा अभी भी नहीं रोता है, तो एक थैले और मास्क के साथ वेंटिलेशन करें।

If the baby is not crying and not even breathing after birth, immediately suction the throat with a mucus extractor. If the baby still doesn’t breathe, suction the nose with a mucus extractor. If the baby still doesn’t cry, perform ventilation with a bag and mask.

02Meconium-stained liquor

प्रसव के समय पानी पीला और गाढ़ा है और बच्चे का सिर दिख रहा है

Why it mattersYellow, thick liquor means the baby has passed meconium in the womb. If it is inhaled at the first breath it blocks the airway, so the mouth must be cleared before the chest delivers.

The baby's head is visible, and the water is yellow and thick. The ASHA should clear the mouth of the baby with gauze as soon as the head is seen.

03Danger signs of newborn sepsis

नवजात में सेप्सिस के क्या लक्षण होते हैं

Why it mattersOne newborn in ten develops sepsis in rural India and it is the commonest killer in the first month. Untreated most die; treated most recover completely, so recognition is the whole battle.

What the device says

नवजात शिशु में सेप्सिस के लक्षणों में अंग लंगड़े होना, खाना खाना बंद करना, छाती में दर्द, बुखार और छूने में ठंड शामिल है।

The symptoms of sepsis in a newborn include limbs becoming limp, stopping feeding, chest indrawing, fever, and being cold to touch.

04When to refer a newborn at once

नवजात को कब तुरंत अस्पताल भेजना चाहिए

Why it mattersKnowing the signs is useless without knowing the threshold to act on them. This is the decision an ASHA has to make alone, at night, far from a facility.

What the device says

यदि निम्नलिखित में से कोई भी संकेत विकसित होता हैः अंग लंगड़े हो जाते हैं, खाना बंद कर देते हैं, छाती में दर्द होता है, बुखार होता है, या छूने में सर्दी होती है तो नवजात को तुरंत अस्पताल भेजा जाना चाहिए।

The newborn should be sent to the hospital immediately if any of the following signs develop: limbs become limp, stops feeding, has chest indrawing, has fever, or is cold to touch.

07High fever with a stiff neck

बच्चे को तेज बुखार है और गर्दन अकड़ गई है

Why it mattersFever with neck stiffness is meningitis until proved otherwise. Hours matter, and the temptation to treat a fever at home is exactly what costs the time.

What the device says

स्मीयर बनाने के बाद मलेरिया रोधी दवा की पहली खुराक दें। तेज बुखार को कम करने के लिए पेरासिटामोल की एक खुराक दें। अस्पताल में रेफरल।

Give first dose of anti-malarial, after making a smear. Give one dose of paracetamol to bring the high fever down. Referral to hospital.

08Bathing a low birth weight newborn

बच्चे का वजन बहुत कम है और परिवार उसे जन्म के तुरंत बाद नहलाना चाहता है

Why it mattersBathing a newborn immediately is a widespread custom. In a small baby it causes hypothermia, which leads to sepsis. The ASHA has to overrule the family with a number, not an opinion.

What the device says

बच्चा बहुत हल्का है और परिवार उसे जन्म के तुरंत बाद नहलाना चाहता है। मार्गदर्शन के अनुसार, बच्चे को तब तक नहलाना न चाहिए जब तक कि उसका वजन 2000 ग्राम न हो जाए। इसलिए, परिवार को नहाने से पहले बच्चे के इस वजन तक पहुंचने तक इंतजार करना चाहिए।

The baby is very light weight and the family wants to bathe him immediately after birth. According to the guidance, do not bathe a baby until its weight is 2000 gm. Therefore, the family should wait until the baby reaches this weight before bathing.

09Dehydration in a child with diarrhoea

दस्त से पीड़ित बच्चे में पानी की कमी के लक्षण क्या हैं

Why it mattersDiarrhoea kills through dehydration, not infection. The signs are physical and checkable at the doorstep, and they decide between ORS at home and urgent referral.

What the device says

दस्त वाले बच्चे में निर्जलीकरण के लक्षणों में सुस्ती, बेहोशी, आंखें डूबना, पीने या खराब तरीके से पीने में सक्षम नहीं होना और त्वचा बहुत धीरे-धीरे वापस चली जाती है।

The symptoms of dehydration in a child with diarrhea include lethargy, unconsciousness, sunken eyes, not being able to drink or drinking poorly, and the skin pinch going back very slowly.

10Feeding a low birth weight baby

कम वजन वाले नवजात को दूध कैसे पिलाना चाहिए

Why it mattersA small baby who feeds poorly loses weight, gets cold and becomes septic. Feeding support in the first days is what breaks that chain.

What the device says

दूध की आपूर्ति को बनाए रखने के लिए हर 2-3 घंटे में दूध का प्रयोग करें, और चूसने में सक्षम होने के बाद बच्चे को जितनी बार हो सके स्तन में डालें।

Express milk every 2-3 hours to keep the milk supply up, and put the baby to breast as often as possible once she is able to suckle.

Two things to know before demonstrating

The translation has two rough edges in this set. "Chest indrawing" comes out as छाती में दर्द, which means chest pain, and "limbs limp" as अंग लंगड़े, which suggests limping rather than floppy. The English is right and the retrieval is right; the Hindi wording is not, and it is a translation-model limit, not a retrieval one.

Speak the question as written. Speech recognition is the weakest link in the chain: one mis-heard syllable once turned a question about an eye infection into one about a child drinking, and the device answered the question it was given. One word decides the meaning.

Recording is a toggle — one press starts, the next ends it, and 3.5 s of silence ends it anyway. Timings above are machine time from the stop, and exclude how long the question itself takes to ask.
